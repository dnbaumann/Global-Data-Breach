                  How to read this

                  • Not legal advice. Laws change, and many have exceptions, thresholds and sector rules this tool does not model (for example NIS2, DORA, GLBA and bank regulators). Confirm every jurisdiction with counsel.
                  • Each entry is tagged Checked (cross-checked against current published sources in October 2026), Established (settled law, not re-checked) or Verify (recent, ambiguous or thinly sourced). Jurisdictions with no entry are shown as having no fixed deadline identified, which is not the same as having no obligation.
                  • Business days exclude Saturdays and Sundays only. Local public holidays are not applied. Day counts keep the same clock time as the starting point.
                  • The notification date is treated as the date both regulators and individuals are told. If you notify them on different days, check each duty on its own.
                  • Incident dates and affected counts never leave this page and are not saved. Only edits to the rules are stored: with the shared rules, in this artifact for everyone who can open it; otherwise in this browser.
